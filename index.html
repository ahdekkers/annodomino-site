Anno Domino Keyword Research

Anno Domino Keyword Research is a keyword research app. It looks up how often people search Google for a phrase, and what an advert on that phrase would cost, so that Anno Domino can decide which software products to build for small businesses.

What Anno Domino Keyword Research does

  • Takes a list of search phrases, such as "rota software" or "job management app".
  • Asks the Google Ads API for each phrase's average monthly searches in the United Kingdom and its estimated cost per click.
  • Saves the figures so that markets can be compared by how many people search for them and what it costs to reach those people.

The app only reads keyword planning data from Anno Domino's own Google Ads account. It does not create, change or manage adverts, and it does not access anyone else's Google Ads account or data.

Who uses it

Anno Domino Keyword Research is an internal research tool, used only by Anno Domino itself. It is not sold or offered to other people.

Your data

The app handles keyword statistics, not personal information. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it reads from Google and how that is stored, and the terms of use.
