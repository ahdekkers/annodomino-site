Anno Domino Keyword Research

An internal research tool used by Anno Domino, a small UK software studio, to understand which kinds of software small businesses search for.

What the app does

Anno Domino Keyword Research connects to the Google Ads API to read keyword planning figures for our own Google Ads account: how many people search for a phrase each month, and the estimated price of a click on it. We use these figures to decide which products are worth building and whether a market is large enough to advertise to.

The app only reads keyword data. It does not create, change or manage ads, and it does not access anyone else's Google Ads account.

Who uses it

The app is for internal use only. It is run by Anno Domino's owner for Anno Domino's own research. There is no sign-up, no customer login and no outside users, so there is nothing to log in to on this site.

About Anno Domino

Anno Domino is a small software studio based in the United Kingdom that builds simple tools for small businesses. It is run by Alexander Dekkers, trading as Anno Domino.
