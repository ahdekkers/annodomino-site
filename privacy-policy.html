Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information Anno Domino Keyword Research (“the app”) and this website collect, how they are used and how they are protected. Anno Domino is a trading name of Alexander Dekkers, United Kingdom, who is responsible for the app and this website.

Who the app is for

The app is an internal tool used only by Anno Domino for its own market research. It has no outside users and no sign-up, and it never asks members of the public to sign in with Google or to grant it access to their accounts.

Information the app accesses from Google

The app uses the Google Ads API to access Anno Domino's own Google Ads account and nothing else. Through it, the app reads keyword planning data only:

  • keyword phrases that Anno Domino submits for research;
  • for each phrase, Google's estimate of average monthly searches, monthly search trends, competition level and top-of-page bid estimates, for the United Kingdom.

The app does not read, create or change ads, campaigns, budgets, billing details or audience lists, and it does not access any other person's or organisation's Google account or data. The keyword data it receives is aggregated by Google and contains no personal information about any individual.

How the information is used

The keyword figures are used solely to research which software products small businesses look for and what advertising to them would cost. They are not used for advertising targeting, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to that research.

Storage and security

  • Keyword phrases and their figures are stored in a private code repository that only Anno Domino can access.
  • The credentials the app uses to reach the Google Ads API are kept in a private secrets store, never in code or on this website.
  • Data is kept for as long as it is useful for research and is deleted on request or when no longer needed.

Sharing

Anno Domino does not sell, rent or share data obtained through Google APIs with anyone. It is not transferred to third parties except where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Anno Domino Keyword Research's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking scripts and has no forms. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process standard technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Emails to us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. The app itself holds no personal information. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
